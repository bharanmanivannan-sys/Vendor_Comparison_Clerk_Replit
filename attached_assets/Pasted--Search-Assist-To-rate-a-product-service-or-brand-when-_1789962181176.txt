Evaluating Products, Services, or Brands
When you cannot verify claims from trusted sources, you can still assess a product, service, or brand by following these guidelines:

Consider Multiple Sources
Use a variety of review platforms to gather a broader perspective.
Look for well-known websites that provide trustworthy and impartial reviews.
Check Review Recency
Ensure that the reviews are recent. A sudden influx of reviews in a short time may indicate manipulation.
Older reviews may not reflect the current quality of the product or service.
Assess Reviewer Credibility
Investigate the reviewers' profiles. Check if they have written multiple reviews or if they seem to be one-time users.
Be cautious of reviews that appear overly positive or negative, as they may not be genuine.
Analyze Review Content
Look for detailed reviews that provide specific information about the product or service.
Reviews that include both pros and cons are often more reliable than those that are entirely positive or negative.
Watch for Incentives
Some reviews may be influenced by incentives. Check if the platform indicates whether the reviewer received a product for free or was compensated.
Use Structured Data
If available, look for structured data that displays ratings and reviews directly on product pages. This can enhance credibility.

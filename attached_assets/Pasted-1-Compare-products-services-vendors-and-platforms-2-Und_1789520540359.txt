CONTEXT UNDERSTANDING
Before comparing vendors, understand:
• Industry
• Business objectives
• Current platform/vendor
• Target platform/vendor
• Regulatory requirements
• Security requirements
• Customer experience goals
• Operational constraints
• Budget constraints
• Time-to-market expectations
• Integration landscape
• Data migration requirements
• Technical maturity
 
If any information is missing, infer likely scenarios and explicitly state assumptions.

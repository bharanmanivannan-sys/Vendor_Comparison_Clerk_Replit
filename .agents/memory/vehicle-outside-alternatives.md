---
name: Vehicle outside alternatives
description: Evidence and compatibility rules for suggesting vehicles outside the compared shortlist.
---

An outside vehicle alternative must be outside every compared name and alias, available in the selected market, and compatible with the established body style and drivetrain. Two validated options are preferred; if two cannot be established, state the coverage gap without naming speculative models.

**Why:** Model-written alternatives can survive as plausible names even when they repeat a compared alias, use the wrong drivetrain or segment, belong to another market, or are no longer available.

**How to apply:** Treat official local product availability as required evidence. Revalidate dated availability rather than allowing stale candidates to remain indefinitely. Preserve an explicit no-invention message through final narrative synthesis.

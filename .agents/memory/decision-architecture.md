---
name: Decision architecture
description: Durable analysis requirements for product, service, vendor, and brand comparisons.
---

Comparisons must explicitly map product or service equivalency, functional gaps, business-service/product arrangements, migration sequencing, and decision governance. Missing business context must be stated as assumptions.

**Why:** A feature-and-price comparison does not establish whether a target arrangement replaces the current operating capability, how the organization can transition safely, or who has authority to approve the decision.

**How to apply:** Use structured fields in research output, saved results, API responses, UI, and exports. Never infer equivalence from product names alone, never recommend solely on cost, explain scores, and prioritize long-term value, risk reduction, and strategic fit.

---
name: Actionable strategic frameworks
description: Quality rule for turning SWOT, PESTLE, SOAR, and similar frameworks into decision support.
---

Strategic-framework findings must be option-specific decision outputs, not instructions for completing the framework. Reject text that tells the reader to “identify” or “define” a strength, opportunity, aspiration, or result.

**Why:** Framework prompts can look structurally complete while giving product managers and buyers no insight. They also survive shallow checks when prefixed with an option name.

**How to apply:** Name the observed differentiator or gap, explain the product-management action and buyer implication, and provide a measurable target, timeframe, or validation method. Preserve substantive researched findings; recover vague legacy findings from the report’s verified scores and comparison rows.

---
name: Customer-facing terminology
description: Terminology and hero-legibility constraints for DecisionIntel’s visible interface.
---

Do not use “signal” in customer-facing navigation, labels, headings, or explanatory copy. Prefer specific terms such as evidence, fit, score, comparison, or recommendation.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly rejected “signal” as visible terminology because it is vague and had remained scattered across landing, guest, and report surfaces.

**How to apply:** Check new or revised UI copy case-insensitively before release. Hero headlines must also use deliberate wrapping and explicit light/dark contrast rather than relying on an oversized display font to wrap safely.
